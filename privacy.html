Life Tracker / Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Life Tracker ("the App", "we", "our") is a personal finance and expense-splitting Progressive Web App built and maintained by Mani Bhushan Kumar. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles, where it lives, and the choices you have — including how the optional Google Drive backup feature works. If anything here is unclear, jump to the Contact section.

Summary (TL;DR)

  • Life Tracker is a local-first, client-side app. There is no account signup, no server backend, and no company database — your data lives on your own device.
  • We do not run analytics, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services in the App. There is nothing "phoning home" in the background.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. There is no third party to share it with — we simply don't collect it centrally.
  • Google Drive backup is 100% optional and, when enabled, only ever touches files the App itself created in your own Drive.

What data the App stores, and where

Everything you enter into Life Tracker is saved directly on your device using your browser's built-in IndexedDB storage — the same on-device technology behind most offline-capable web apps. Depending on how you use the App, this may include:

  • Expenses & categories — amounts, dates, notes, and category labels you record.
  • Recurring / due items — bills or items you've set up to track.
  • Splitwise-style shared expenses — group names, the names or nicknames of people you split costs with (whatever you type in — we never pull this from your contacts or any external source), and running balances.
  • App settings — preferences like your currency, categories, and the Google OAuth Client ID you optionally provide for Drive backup (stored locally only, never transmitted to us).

None of this data is transmitted to Mani Bhushan Kumar, to any server we operate, or to any analytics/advertising company — because no such server exists. If you uninstall the App or clear your browser's site data, this information is permanently deleted along with it (which is also why the backup feature below exists).

Optional Google Drive backup & sync

Life Tracker offers an optional feature to back up your data to your own Google Drive account so you don't lose it if you switch devices or clear your browser. This feature is off by default and only activates if you explicitly connect your Google account from the Settings page.

  • Scope requested: drive.file — Google's most restrictive Drive scope. This grants the App access only to the specific files/folder it creates itself (a folder named "Life-Tracker" containing your backup JSON files). The App cannot see, list, read, or modify any other file already in your Drive.
  • What gets uploaded: the same expenses, settings, and splitwise data described above, saved as JSON files inside that one folder in your own Drive — nothing more.
  • Who can access it: only you. The backup lives in your personal Google Drive; we (the developer) have no server-side copy and no ability to view, download, or access your Drive files. Authentication happens entirely client-side via Google's Identity Services — your Google credentials are never seen by, or sent to, the App's developer.
  • Your control: you can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings, and you can permanently delete all backup files from your Drive using the "Delete All Drive Backups" option in the Settings danger zone. Restoring a backup is always a manual, deliberate action you trigger yourself.

Life Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • No account creation, no email/phone collection, no login system of our own.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with third parties or advertisers.
  • No use of your Google Drive data for anything other than storing/retrieving your own backups — it's never read for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's core backup function.

Cookies, caching & offline support

Life Tracker is a Progressive Web App and uses a Service Worker plus localStorage purely to cache app files (HTML/CSS/JS) so it works offline, and to remember small UI preferences like which tab you last had open. None of this is used for tracking, fingerprinting, or advertising — it exists solely to make the App fast and usable without an internet connection.

Children's privacy

Life Tracker is a general-purpose expense tracker not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App itself never collects any data on our servers (there are none), there is no data of any user, of any age, held by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future feature introduces new data handling — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will change accordingly. Since there are no user accounts, we have no way to email you directly, so please check back here if you have concerns.
